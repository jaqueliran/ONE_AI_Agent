# Attendance & Make-up Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines Common Ground's rules concerning student attendance, missed classes, and make-up activities.

It establishes:

* minimum attendance requirements;
* procedures for justified and unjustified absences;
* make-up options;
* deadlines;
* limits on make-up activities;
* exceptional situations.

This policy complements the **Academic Policy**, **Student Progression & Placement Policy**, and **Assessment Policy**.

---

## 2. Minimum Attendance Requirement

Students are expected to attend at least **75% of their scheduled classes** during a course or module.

Attendance is considered part of the student's academic participation.

Meeting the minimum attendance requirement does not automatically guarantee progression. Students must also meet the academic and assessment requirements established in the relevant policies.

Students who fall below the minimum attendance requirement may become ineligible for progression unless an applicable exception or approved academic procedure applies.

---

## 3. Attendance Records

Attendance is recorded by the teacher for each scheduled class.

Students are responsible for checking their attendance records and communicating possible errors to the teacher or Academic Coordination.

Attendance records may be used when determining academic eligibility and progression.

---

## 4. Justified Absences

An absence may be considered justified when circumstances reasonably prevent the student from attending a scheduled class.

Examples may include:

* illness;
* family emergencies;
* significant personal emergencies;
* previously authorized academic activities;
* other exceptional circumstances accepted by Academic Coordination.

A justified absence does not automatically remove the absence from the attendance record.

Instead, it may allow the student to request an appropriate make-up activity or other academic accommodation when applicable.

---

## 5. Unjustified Absences

An absence is considered unjustified when the student does not attend a scheduled class and does not provide an accepted reason or follow the applicable notification procedure.

Unjustified absences count toward the student's attendance record.

Repeated unjustified absences may result in academic follow-up by the teacher or Academic Coordination.

---

## 6. Notification of Absence

Students should notify the school or teacher as soon as reasonably possible when they know they will miss a class.

For unexpected absences, notification should be provided as soon as the student is able to do so.

Notification alone does not guarantee that an absence will be considered justified.

---

## 7. Make-up Activities

Make-up activities are intended to help students recover learning opportunities lost because of an absence.

Depending on the course and circumstances, a make-up activity may include:

* an alternative classroom activity;
* an individual assignment;
* a recorded learning activity;
* a guided practice task;
* a short assessment;
* another equivalent activity approved by the teacher.

A make-up activity should address the relevant learning objectives of the missed class whenever reasonably possible.

Make-up activities are not intended to reproduce the entire missed class.

---

## 8. Limits on Make-up Activities

Make-up activities do not automatically restore attendance as if the student had attended the original class.

A student may complete a make-up activity to recover relevant learning opportunities, but the original absence remains part of the attendance record unless an approved exception applies.

The school may limit the number or type of make-up activities available when repeated absences interfere with normal participation in the course.

Students who frequently rely on make-up activities may be referred to Academic Coordination for academic follow-up.

---

## 9. Deadlines for Make-up Requests

Students should request a make-up activity within **seven calendar days** of the missed class.

Requests submitted after this period may be considered only when the student provides a relevant reason for the delay or when an exceptional circumstance applies.

The teacher or Academic Coordination may establish a different deadline when necessary because of the structure or timing of a course.

---

## 10. Alternative Make-up Formats

When appropriate, Common Ground may provide alternative formats for recovering missed learning.

These may include:

* asynchronous activities;
* written assignments;
* recorded materials;
* individual meetings;
* additional practice;
* alternative assessment activities.

The format should be appropriate to the learning objectives and should provide reasonable evidence that the student engaged with the missed content.

---

## 11. Missed Assessments

Missing an assessment does not automatically result in a make-up opportunity.

When a student misses a required assessment, the applicable procedures in the **Assessment Policy** should be followed.

Depending on the circumstances, the student may be offered a recovery or alternative assessment.

Attendance and assessment eligibility remain separate requirements.

---

## 12. Exceptional Situations

Academic Coordination may review exceptional attendance cases individually.

Exceptional situations may include:

* serious personal circumstances;
* prolonged illness;
* significant family emergencies;
* circumstances that substantially affect the student's ability to attend classes;
* other situations that require an individualized academic decision.

When reviewing an exceptional case, Academic Coordination may consider:

* the student's attendance history;
* the duration and nature of the circumstances;
* academic performance;
* completed assessments;
* available evidence of learning;
* the student's ability to continue successfully in the course.

An exception should be documented when appropriate.

---

## 13. Attendance and Progression

Attendance is one of the factors considered when determining academic progression.

A student who meets the academic requirements but does not meet the minimum attendance requirement may not be eligible to progress.

In exceptional circumstances, Academic Coordination may review the student's case according to the procedures described in this policy.

For detailed progression criteria, refer to the **Student Progression & Placement Policy**.

---

## 14. Roles and Responsibilities

### Students

Students are responsible for:

* attending scheduled classes;
* notifying the school of absences when possible;
* requesting make-up activities within the applicable deadline;
* completing approved make-up activities;
* checking their attendance records;
* communicating attendance concerns promptly.

### Teachers

Teachers are responsible for:

* recording attendance accurately;
* communicating relevant attendance concerns;
* providing or assigning appropriate make-up activities when applicable;
* documenting completed make-up activities;
* referring recurring attendance concerns to Academic Coordination.

### Academic Coordination

Academic Coordination is responsible for:

* reviewing exceptional attendance cases;
* supporting decisions involving repeated absences;
* reviewing requests that fall outside standard procedures;
* ensuring consistency between attendance and academic progression procedures.

---

## 15. Relationship with Other Policies

This policy should be read together with:

* **Academic Policy** for general academic procedures;
* **Student Progression & Placement Policy** for level progression and academic advancement;
* **Assessment Policy** for assessment procedures, grading, recovery, and feedback;
* **Teacher Handbook** for classroom practices and teacher responsibilities.

When a question concerns assessment, the Assessment Policy should be consulted.

When a question concerns progression, the Student Progression & Placement Policy should be consulted.

When a question concerns classroom procedures, the Teacher Handbook should be consulted.
